# Practical UX research planning

## When to use
Use when important design choices depend on uncertain user behavior.

## Workflow
1. List the riskiest assumptions and decisions they influence.
2. Choose the lightest credible method: interviews, usability tests, analytics review, survey, or support-ticket analysis.
3. Write neutral research questions and non-leading task prompts.
4. Define participants, sample limitations, consent/privacy considerations, and what evidence would change the decision.
5. Separate observed evidence, interpretation, and recommendation.

## Expected outputs
- Research plan
- Questions/tasks
- Evidence log structure
- Decision rule

## Avoid
- Invented interview results or quotes
- Surveys for questions better answered by observing behavior

## Done when
- The recommendations connect to the actual product goal and user task.
- Important assumptions and unverified claims are explicitly labeled.
- The result is specific enough to implement or evaluate.
