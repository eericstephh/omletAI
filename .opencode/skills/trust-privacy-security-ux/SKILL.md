# Trust, privacy, and security UX

## When to use
Use for account, permission, payment, sharing, and sensitive settings flows.

## Workflow
1. Explain why sensitive information or permissions are needed at the moment of request.
2. Make sharing scope, audience, retention, and consequences understandable.
3. Use confirmation for consequential actions and provide clear recovery where possible.
4. Make security settings and session/account actions discoverable.
5. Do not use manipulative consent patterns or hide privacy-relevant choices.

## Expected outputs
- Trust-risk review
- Permission and sharing copy
- Confirmation/recovery behavior
- Transparency gaps

## Avoid
- Dark patterns or coercive consent
- Vague privacy language

## Done when
- The recommendations connect to the actual product goal and user task.
- Important assumptions and unverified claims are explicitly labeled.
- The result is specific enough to implement or evaluate.
