# Pricing, plans, and subscription UX

## When to use
Use for subscription selection, plan comparison, upgrades, and cancellation.

## Workflow
1. Explain limits, billing cadence, renewal, trial terms, and cancellation consequences clearly.
2. Compare plans on criteria users care about and avoid feature-table overload.
3. Make current plan, usage, renewal date, and upgrade/downgrade consequences visible.
4. Support cancellation and recovery without dark patterns.
5. Ensure displayed promises match actual entitlement and billing behavior.

## Expected outputs
- Plan comparison hierarchy
- Billing/usage states
- Upgrade/downgrade flow
- Transparency checklist

## Avoid
- Hidden fees or confusing renewal language
- Obstructive cancellation flows

## Done when
- The recommendations connect to the actual product goal and user task.
- Important assumptions and unverified claims are explicitly labeled.
- The result is specific enough to implement or evaluate.
