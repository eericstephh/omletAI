# Onboarding and first-run experience

## When to use
Use when users need setup, education, permissions, or an initial successful outcome.

## Workflow
1. Identify the earliest valuable outcome and get users there quickly.
2. Request permissions or account setup only when context makes the benefit clear.
3. Teach through progressive disclosure and task-based guidance, not long tours.
4. Provide skip, defer, resume, and recovery paths where feasible.
5. Measure proposed onboarding success by task completion and later friction, not tutorial completion alone.

## Expected outputs
- First-run flow
- Permission timing
- Skip/resume behavior
- Success criteria

## Avoid
- Blocking the product behind unnecessary tutorials
- Requesting permissions before explaining why

## Done when
- The recommendations connect to the actual product goal and user task.
- Important assumptions and unverified claims are explicitly labeled.
- The result is specific enough to implement or evaluate.
