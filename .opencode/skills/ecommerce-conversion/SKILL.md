# Ecommerce and conversion UX

## When to use
Use for product discovery, product details, carts, and checkout.

## Workflow
1. Make product information, price, availability, shipping, returns, and variants clear.
2. Support comparison and decision-making without overwhelming users.
3. Reduce checkout friction while preserving trust, confirmation, and user control.
4. Show validation errors inline and preserve form progress.
5. Treat conversion improvements as hypotheses to test, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Expected outputs
- Purchase journey
- Trust/friction review
- Checkout state matrix
- Experiment hypotheses

## Avoid
- Fake urgency or deceptive scarcity
- Hidden fees or preselected costly add-ons

## Done when
- The recommendations connect to the actual product goal and user task.
- Important assumptions and unverified claims are explicitly labeled.
- The result is specific enough to implement or evaluate.
