# Landing pages and product marketing UI

## When to use
Use for product pages, feature pages, launches, and campaign pages.

## Workflow
1. Match the page's promise to the target audience and intended action.
2. Make value proposition, proof, product detail, objection handling, and CTA hierarchy coherent.
3. Use real evidence and authentic product imagery; never invent customer logos or metrics.
4. Keep sections purposeful and remove repeated claims that add no information.
5. Check mobile hierarchy, load performance, and consistency between promise and product.

## Expected outputs
- Page narrative
- Section hierarchy
- CTA strategy
- Evidence gaps

## Avoid
- Generic hero copy that could describe any product
- Unsupported testimonials, logos, or performance claims

## Done when
- The recommendations connect to the actual product goal and user task.
- Important assumptions and unverified claims are explicitly labeled.
- The result is specific enough to implement or evaluate.
